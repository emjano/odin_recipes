Pasta Carbonara

Description

Pasta Carbonara is a delicious italian dish

Ingredients

  • Guanciale
  • Garlic
  • Spaghetti
  • 4 Eggs
  • Grated Cheese
  • Virgin oil

Steps

  1. Fry the guanciale and add the garlic in the end.
  2. Boil the pasta during, take away 1 1/2 dl of the pastawater after half of the cookingtime.
  3. Whip the eggyolks and grated cheese with 2-3 msk cooled pasta water(DO NOT USE HOT).
  4. Turn down the heat on the stove and put the pasta with the guanciale and garlic, then add the egg and cheese, very important to not have the pan too hot.
  5. Add pasta water to get a creamy texture.
  6. Add salt to your taste, then add a bunch of black pepper.
